## Make culture visible

Talent looks for concrete proof: teams, projects, growth, atmosphere, expectations and perspectives.

## Structure content

A useful employer brand combines portraits, behind the scenes, open roles, a career page, social content and targeted campaigns.

## Measure attractiveness

Digital makes it possible to track applications, sources, profiles, costs and conversion rates until recruitment.

## Show what candidates want to verify

Candidates do not only read a job description. They want to understand the atmosphere, missions, level of expectation, projects, managers, growth opportunities and credibility of the company. Digital makes these elements visible before the first conversation.

A career page, team portraits, short videos, LinkedIn content, behind-the-scenes moments and testimonials provide proof. The goal is not to create a perfect image, but to show a clear and consistent culture.

## Connect content, jobs and conversion

Effective employer branding is not just posting team photos. It structures a journey: attract, reassure, explain, guide and convert into an application. Content should [lead](/glossaire/lead) to clear roles and a simple application experience.

For companies in Morocco or abroad, messages also need to be localized: cities, roles, culture, languages, work rhythm and candidate expectations. Recruitment campaigns perform better when the message speaks precisely to the profiles being targeted.

## Measure attractiveness over time

Employer brand is built through repetition. Teams should track application sources, profile quality, cost, interview rates, drop-offs and candidate feedback. These signals show what really attracts talent.
