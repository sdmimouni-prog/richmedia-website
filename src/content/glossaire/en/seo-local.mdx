Local SEO matters for agencies, clinics, schools, restaurants, stores and service providers. In Morocco, searches often include city names such as Casablanca, Rabat or Marrakech.
The mistake is creating location pages without real local proof. Address, reviews, services, photos and consistent information are essential.
