A brand uses digital acquisition to define where demand will come from and how each channel contributes. In Morocco, this often means combining Google demand, Meta discovery, WhatsApp follow-up and landing pages built for conversion.
The common mistake is to buy traffic before clarifying the offer, audience and tracking. Acquisition only becomes profitable when the funnel, message and measurement system work together.
