Google Ads is useful when people already express demand through search or when video and display can support awareness. In Morocco, it often complements SEO on competitive commercial queries.
The pitfall is launching campaigns without conversion tracking or landing pages. Paid visibility needs measurement and post-click quality to become profitable.
