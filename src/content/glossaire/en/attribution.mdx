Attribution helps teams avoid judging performance from the last click only. For a Moroccan campaign, a prospect might discover the brand on Meta, search it on Google, then convert after a WhatsApp reminder.
The risk is treating attribution as absolute truth. Tracking limits, cookie loss and offline behavior mean attribution should guide decisions, not replace business judgment.
