CPL is useful for lead generation campaigns in real estate, education, automotive or B2B services. It gives a simple view of acquisition cost.
The pitfall is celebrating a low CPL while sales teams receive weak contacts. Lead quality, source, intent and follow-up speed matter as much as the number.
