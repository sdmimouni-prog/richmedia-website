A CRM helps a team know who contacted the brand, what they asked for and what should happen next. It is essential when leads arrive from forms, WhatsApp, calls and media campaigns.
The mistake is treating CRM as a storage tool only. Its value comes from clean data, clear stages, useful automation and disciplined sales follow-up.
